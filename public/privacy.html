eChopdo — Privacy

eChopdo is a private family app for one household's money records, passwords and papers. It is not offered to the public.

What is stored

Budget and Hisab records are stored in the owner's own Supabase database. Passwords, the Emergency sheet and the Will are encrypted on the device before they are stored, so the database holds only unreadable data.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Signing in with Google only identifies the owner. If the owner turns on backups, eChopdo asks for the limited Google Drive permission drive.file. It can then create and manage only the backup files it makes itself, in a folder called “eChopdo backups”. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything other than running the app and the owner's backups.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect backups in eChopdo (Settings → Backup to Google Drive) or remove eChopdo at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files already in Google Drive can be deleted from the Drive.
